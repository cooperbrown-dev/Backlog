One open design decision

Category duplicates the TPH discriminator — once a row is a VideoGame, EF already records its type, so a separate Category enum is double-bookkeeping that can drift. Pick one: keep the subtypes and drop Category, or keep a flat BacklogItem with Category and drop the subtypes.
